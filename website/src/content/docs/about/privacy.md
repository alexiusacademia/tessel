## Tessel itself

Tessel counts its installations by sending a small message to
`stats.syncster.dev`: a random id that names the installation and not you,
the version, and the kind of system. Nothing else is sent, and
`tessel telemetry off` turns it off. [What Tessel reports](/start/installation/#what-tessel-reports)
lists it in full.

The programs and apps you build with Tessel send nothing, and show no ads.

## Ads on this website

This website shows ads from Google AdSense. To choose and count the ads,
Google and its partners set cookies in your browser and may show ads based
on your visits to this and other websites. Google explains this in
[How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
You can turn off personalised ads in [My Ad Center](https://myadcenter.google.com/),
or block the cookies in your browser; the website works the same without
them.

The website has no analytics of its own. Like any website, its host keeps
ordinary server logs.
